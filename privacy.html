Solyr ix Anti-Nuke Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Solyr ix Anti-Nuke Bot (“the Bot”) may process and why.

1. Information Processed

When enabled by a server administrator, the Bot may process server ID and name, channel and category names, channel settings and permissions, role information, audit-log events, member IDs and names involved in moderation events, message text included in configured backups, and attachment URLs included in those backups.

2. Why Information Is Used

This information is used only to detect suspicious server activity, apply configured protective actions, create server snapshots, and recreate missing channels during recovery.

3. Storage and Retention

Backups are stored by the Bot operator in local files or the hosting service used to run the Bot. Server administrators control when backups are created and may delete backup files from the Bot’s storage. The Bot does not sell personal information.

4. Discord Data

The Bot uses Discord’s API and may receive information permitted by its enabled intents and server permissions. Discord’s own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply.

5. Your Choices

A server administrator can remove the Bot, disable backups, or delete stored backup files. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Bot owner through the Discord account that manages this application.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features, storage, or data handling changes.
